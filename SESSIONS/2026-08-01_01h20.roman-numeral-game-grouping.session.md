# Session: Roman Numeral Game Title Grouping

Date: 2026-08-01 01:20

## Objective
Ensure that titles using Roman numerals (e.g., `Streets of Rage II`) and titles using Arabic numerals (e.g., `Streets of Rage 2`) are recognized and grouped as the same game.

## Context & Root Cause
In `src/core/rom-grouping.ts`:
1. `normalizeGameName` does not convert Roman numerals to Arabic numerals, producing different group identifiers (`Streets of Rage II` vs `Streets of Rage 2`).
2. `groupDatGames` groups raw titles strictly by `entry.parsed.title`. When two DAT entries have different title strings (`Streets of Rage II` vs `Streets of Rage 2`), it detects a title collision (`titles.size > 1`) and splits them into distinct `GameGroup` items with checksum suffixes.

## Proposed Changes

1. **Roman Numeral Normalization in `src/core/rom-grouping.ts`**:
   - Implement `normalizeRomanNumerals(text: string): string` to convert standalone Roman numerals (`II` through `XXX`) to Arabic numerals (`2` through `30`) using word-boundary matching.
   - Use `normalizeRomanNumerals` inside `normalizeGameName`.
2. **Canonical Title Matching in `groupDatGames`**:
   - Update `groupDatGames` to compare titles by a canonical key (`normalizeTitle`), allowing `Streets of Rage II` and `Streets of Rage 2` to group under the same title bucket.
   - Preserve collision detection for titles that differ in characters other than Roman numerals / safe filename sanitization (e.g. `Ys III: Wanderers` vs `Ys III_ Wanderers`).
3. **Unit Tests in `src/core/rom-grouping.test.ts`**:
   - Add unit tests verifying `normalizeRomanNumerals` converts `Streets of Rage II` to `Streets of Rage 2`.
   - Add unit test in `groupDatGames` confirming `Streets of Rage II (USA)` and `Streets of Rage 2 (Europe)` are grouped into a single `GameGroup`.
   - Ensure all 321 unit tests continue to pass.
